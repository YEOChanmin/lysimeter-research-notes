# Evidence and Limitations

| Evidence | What it supports | What requires separate evidence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Local synthetic test | Expected behavior for defined inputs | Real sensor, cloud and device integration |
| Independent code review | Findings and correction checks for a specified version and scope | A guarantee of no defects under all conditions |
| Device acknowledgment or report | The device reports acceptance, completion or an OFF state | Physical inflow, physical stopping and measured volume |
| Field observation | Conditions observed at a particular time | Long-term reliability and quantitative performance |
| Hash and restoration comparison | Matching files and restoration behavior for a specified package | Completeness across every date and data type |
| Research comparison | Results under specified methods, samples and conditions | Generalization to other settings |

## Scope of this edition

These pages were edited from local records. Preparing them involved no operational queries, field retesting, application execution or USB-backup comparisons. Historical recorded outcomes are distinct from direct checks performed for this documentation package.

The 19 October D-19 result concerns a subset of that day's records, not completion of a full-day backup. The 20 October r7b r2 notes report fixes and tests; independent confirmation and deployment were unconfirmed in the records read for this edition.

When explanations change across records, separate observations from hypotheses and identify the later evidence. A PASS from a different date or version does not automatically apply to a new candidate. No validated crop-productivity, water-use-efficiency or sensor-accuracy performance figures are presented here.
