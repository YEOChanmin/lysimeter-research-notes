# Methods Notes

These notes describe the distinctions needed before turning field observations into research claims. They are not a complete experimental protocol or a statement of validated results.

1. [Measurement and quality](measurement-and-quality.md): origins, units and interventions.
2. [Evidence and limitations](evidence-and-limitations.md): what each form of verification supports.
3. [System architecture](../docs/ARCHITECTURE.md): the path from sensing to records.

Key terms include raw data, calibration, exclusion interval, device report, nominal accounted volume and measured volume.

Literature-grounded equations, crop-specific thresholds and performance comparisons should be added only with verified sources. This edition introduces no new evapotranspiration, biomass or TDS-conversion formula.
